# First pilot results (n = 4)

Informal, formative pilot on 15 November 2026: four adults, facilitated sessions of about 20 minutes, builds 0.3.0 to 0.4.2. Each person read a long article on the normal page and with Speakmi, then completed ratings, the System Usability Scale (SUS) and open comments.

**This does not show that Speakmi improves comprehension.** Four participants, a convenience sample, no comprehension measure, a mix of builds, and in the one session that recorded timings the same article was read in both conditions.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| SUS (0 to 100) | mean 63.1, median 56.3, range 42.5 to 97.5 (commonly cited average: 68) |
| Perceived focus, normal vs Speakmi (1 to 5) | 3.50 vs 3.75; mean difference +0.25, 95% CI -1.27 to +1.77 |
| Perceived mental effort, normal vs Speakmi (1 to 5) | 3.50 vs 3.00; mean difference -0.50, 95% CI -2.55 to +1.55 |
| Would keep using | yes 2, maybe 2, no 0 |
| Anything changed meaning? | no 3; one "yes" box ticked, the comment was a preference for the original, not a distortion |
| Feature ratings (1 to 5) | comfort of text and colours 4.5; controls without help 4.0; Source button 3.5; progress and short parts 3.25; trust in the key points 3.25 (range 2 to 5) |

## What we are changing
Settings are hard to find (first-run guidance), progress is easy to lose on long pages (persistent indicator), trust in the key points is the weakest score (make the fidelity summary and Source button more prominent), and some readers prefer the original page (keep the one-click original view; test per-reader defaults). The pilot used the extractive core only: **no AI rewriting was part of it.**
