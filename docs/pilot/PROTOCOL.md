# Pilot protocol (first small pilot, about 20 minutes per person)

This is a small qualitative pilot, not a clinical study.

1. **Who:** adults who self-report ADHD. No diagnosis is verified or recorded. Informed consent first. Use P1, P2, ... instead of names. Ask separately for permission to quote.
2. **Materials:** two comparable articles of about 1,000 words, each with 3 comprehension questions written in advance. Alternate which article is read with Speakmi.
3. **Steps:** short introduction; read article 1 in the normal view and note the time; answer its questions; read article 2 with Speakmi and note the time; answer its questions.
4. **Ask:** focus rating from 1 to 5 after each reading; at the end whether they would keep using it (yes / maybe / no), what helped, what annoyed them, and anything that looked wrong or changed the meaning.
5. **Record:** order, times, correct answers, ratings, "I lost the thread" presses, bugs, one quote.
6. **Tell participants** it is a prototype, not medical advice, and that they can stop at any time.
7. **Report everything**, including results that look bad. No one is dropped.
